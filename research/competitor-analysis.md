## PART 2: RT CEU REQUIREMENTS BY STATE

### National Overview
- **States licensing RTs:** ~48 + DC
- **States without RT licensure:** Alaska (minimal), Hawaii (limited)
- **Total US RT workforce:** ~150,000-170,000 licensed RTs
- **NBRC credentials:** 3-year renewal cycle (separate from state CEUs)

### Priority States

#### 🔴 TEXAS (Primary Market — ~19-21K RTs)
- **Board:** Texas Board for Respiratory Care (under TDLR)
- **CEUs:** 30 contact hours / 2 years
- **Submission:** Online via TDLR portal, CE Tracker recommended
- **Audit:** Random audits conducted
- **Renewal:** Last day of birth month, every 2 years
- **Retention:** Maintain documentation 5 years
- **2026 change:** TMB now requires use of a tracker, suggesting CE Tracker

#### 🔴 FLORIDA (~15-18K RTs)
- **CEUs:** 24 / 2 years
- **Mandatory topics:** 1hr Medical Errors, 2hr HIV/AIDS, 1hr Domestic Violence
- **Submission:** Online via MQA portal (CE Broker used)
- **CE Tracker Status:** Not required

#### 🔴 CALIFORNIA (~30-35K RTs — largest market)
- **No state RT license** — relies entirely on NBRC credentials
- All governed by NBRC 3-year renewal cycle
- No state audit risk but 100% NBRC compliance risk

#### 🔴 NEW YORK
- **Board:** NYSDOH Office of the Professions
- CEU requirements apply, online renewal

#### Other States (General Pattern)
- Most states: 20-30 CEUs per 2-year cycle
- Varies by state on mandatory topics, submission method, audit frequency
- Multi-state licensure common for travel RTs

### National Credentials
- **NBRC:** RRT, CRT, NPS, ACCS, CPFT, AE-C — 3-year renewal cycle
- **AARC:** Provides CE courses through AARC Learning Network, no public API
- **State boards:** Most accept any documentation method (paper, spreadsheet, self-certification)
