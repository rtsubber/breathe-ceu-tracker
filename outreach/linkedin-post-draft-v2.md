## Main Post

I'm an RRT. CE Broker took 3 days to verify my Texas license.

SB 912 mandates digital CE verification for every Texas RT by September 2026.

What does this mean? September 1, we all get to experience this. Uuggghhh.

So I built Breathe. Here's what onboarding actually looks like — start to finish.

**Step 1: Create your account**
Go to breathe.sublettlabs.com. Enter your email. Pick a password. Done. Account created.

**Step 2: Enter your name**
Type your name. Breathe pulls up your license from the state board automatically. No looking up your license number. No typing it wrong. It just finds you.

**Step 3: Want us to track your NBRC too?**
Same thing. Enter your NBRC login. Breathe pulls your credentials, your 5-year CMP cycle, your assessment status. Now both sides are tracked — state license AND national credential. One dashboard.

**BOOM.** You're done. Ready to track every CEU you earn.

**Then the real magic — adding CEUs:**

📱 **Snap a photo.** Point your phone at a CEU certificate. Breathe's OCR reads it — course title, provider, credits, date — all extracted automatically. No typing.

📧 **Forward the email.** Got a CEU confirmation from AARC or a course provider? Forward it to your Breathe address. Parsed and logged.

🔄 **Auto-sync to CE Broker.** Every CEU you track in Breathe gets pushed to CE Broker automatically. You don't enter anything twice. Ever.

The whole thing — account to tracking CEUs on both your state and national side — takes under 2 minutes.

CEU tracking that actually works. Free forever. $20/year to stop doing it manually.
